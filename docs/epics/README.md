# Joe Perks Platform - Epics Overview

## Project Vision
Joe Perks is a coffee fundraising platform that connects organizations (schools, sports teams, clubs) with local coffee roasters to create mutually beneficial partnerships. Organizations raise funds through coffee sales while roasters expand their customer base and support their communities.

## User Roles & Personas

### 1. Organization Users
**Primary Personas:**
- **Organization Admin**: School principals, PTA presidents, team coaches who set up and manage fundraising campaigns
- **Campaign Manager**: Staff members who manage day-to-day campaign operations and member coordination
- **Organization Member**: Parents, students, supporters who participate in fundraising campaigns

**Key Characteristics:**
- Limited technical expertise, need simple interfaces
- Focused on fundraising goals and community engagement
- Need transparency in revenue sharing and campaign performance
- Require tools for member management and communication

### 2. Roaster Users
**Primary Personas:**
- **Roaster Owner/Admin**: Business owners who manage partnerships and oversee operations
- **Roaster Staff**: Employees who handle order fulfillment and customer service
- **Production Manager**: Staff who manage inventory and shipping logistics

**Key Characteristics:**
- Small to medium business operators with varying technical skills
- Need efficient order management and fulfillment tools
- Require integration with existing business systems (shipping, inventory)
- Focus on maintaining product quality and customer satisfaction

### 3. Platform Admin Users
**Primary Personas:**
- **Platform Administrator**: Joe Perks staff who oversee platform operations
- **Customer Support**: Staff who handle disputes and user support
- **Business Analyst**: Staff who monitor platform performance and growth

**Key Characteristics:**
- High technical proficiency with admin tools
- Need comprehensive oversight and monitoring capabilities
- Require dispute resolution and user management tools
- Focus on platform growth, health, and user satisfaction

## Development Approach

### Epic Development Process
1. **Epic Definition**: Define scope, objectives, and success criteria
2. **User Story Creation**: Break down epics into detailed user stories
3. **Story Estimation**: Size stories using story points
4. **Sprint Planning**: Organize stories into development sprints
5. **Implementation**: Develop, test, and deploy features
6. **Validation**: Measure success against defined criteria

### Cross-Epic Dependencies
- Authentication system required for all user portals
- Payment processing affects organization and roaster experiences
- Order management spans roaster fulfillment and platform oversight
- Analytics data flows between all system components
